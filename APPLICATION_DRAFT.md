## Step 2 · Your idea

### ideas

A job board helps you find a role. The next question is whether it is worth an evening of prep. I built Worth Your Time to help candidates make that call, especially for first-hire roles where scope, equity, runway and success criteria can be hard to compare.

Paste a posting or a speedrun job link and get a short brief: what the role asks you to own, the evidence behind each signal or risk, and questions you can take into the first call. A visible score ledger shows what moved the assessment. Sparse postings get “Not enough to judge.” The tool runs in the browser, and worked examples let anyone try it immediately.

The same checklist gives founders a template for an honest first-hire brief they can share anywhere. I’d start by asking candidates whether the brief surfaced a question they used or saved them time deciding. For founders, I’d test whether publishing a clearer brief reduces repeat screening questions.
