How the assessment works

The score reflects what the posting tells you and where it leaves you guessing. Use the brief to decide what to ask.

Scoring

The score starts at 50. Each criterion adds points for a Signal, subtracts points for a Risk, or adds zero for Unknown. The final score is capped at 0 and 100.

  • 70–100 · Worth a conversation
  • 50–69 · Worth it if they answer the open questions
  • 0–49 · Proceed with caution

Postings under 350 characters or with fewer than three core facts get “Not enough to judge.” Confidence is based on observed criteria. Five or more gives medium confidence, and nine or more gives high. Short or sparse postings always have low confidence.

Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. Only speedrun board URLs call their public, no-auth jobs API. The last pasted posting and founder draft are stored on this device. A paste share link contains the posting text in its fragment, which anyone with the link can decode.
