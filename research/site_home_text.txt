Frequently Asked
Questions, Before You Tell Us Yours.
What Is The Talent Network?
It's a way for talented operators, engineers, designers, AI researchers, and founders-in-waiting to get on a
speedrun
partner's radar without cold-pitching every job listing. You fill out a few questions, and we introduce you to the founders in our portfolio most likely to need you.
How Does It Actually Work?
We map you against the 300+ companies in the
speedrun
portfolio, looking at function, stage, location, and what you've built. When there's a fit, we make a warm intro to the founder, with context on why we routed you to them.
How Much Does It Cost?
Free for candidates. Always. And here's the deeper reason why: we're investors in these companies, not a recruiting agency. We don't take a placement fee, a percentage, or a referral cut. We aren't extracting value from either side. Our incentives are aligned with the founders we back, which means helping them hire the best talent is part of how the whole portfolio wins. Most recruiting orgs make money when a placement happens; we make money when the company succeeds. That's a fundamentally different relationship.
Do I Pick Which Companies I Get Introduced To?
No, and that's the point. We do the matching. You tell us what kind of role and stage you want, and we route you to founders who are actually hiring for that shape of person right now. That said, it's genuinely helpful if you've spent a few minutes browsing the portfolio and have a sense of the kinds of companies that excite you. The strongest matches tend to come from candidates who have at least some clarity about what they're looking for, even if they don't know which specific company is the fit.
What Roles And Stages Do You Place For?
Mostly very early. Our portfolio companies range from 3 people to about 75, but the average is more like 5 to 15. Many are still hunting for product-market fit. That means the work is high-leverage, ambiguous, and shaped by you as much as by the founder. If you want a defined ladder and a 1000-person org, this isn't that. If "early, scrappy, build it from nothing" is the kind of work that excites you, you're in the right place. Roles span engineering, AI research, design, product, marketing, growth, sales, BD, ops, recruiting, and founding-team seats.
What If I'm Already Happily Employed?
Sign up anyway. Many of our best placements happened months after the initial signup, when the right founder finally showed up with the right ask. Staying in the pool costs you nothing.
Is My Information Shared With Companies Before I Agree?
No. We use what you send to decide who you'd be a fit for, then we ask before we make any introduction. You always get to opt in to a specific intro before your name shows up in a founder's inbox.
I Want To Start A Company. Can I Still Sign Up?
Yes. We flag founders-in-waiting separately and route you to
speedrun
partners who can talk about the program, the cohort, and timing. If you're closer to joining-the-speedrun-cohort territory than joining-a-portfolio-company territory, we'll point you there.
If I'm A Student, Can I Sign Up?
Yes. We track internships and new grad roles across the portfolio and the wider tech market, so you can see what's actually open in one place. And we very often introduce college-aged talent (currently in school or about to graduate) directly to founding teams. The expectation there is very low guidance, very high expectations, and chaotic early-stage work. If that excites you, sign up.
How Do I Update My Info?
Just fill out the signup again. We dedupe on email and LinkedIn, so resubmitting overwrites your record with whatever's latest. New location, new function, new portfolio link, all of it. It's the same flow you used the first time, takes a minute.
