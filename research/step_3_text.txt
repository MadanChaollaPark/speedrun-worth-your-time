Where This Could Go
There are a lot of directions we could take this. The goal is to help both founders and job seekers, and a handful of products out there have really inspired us.
For job seekers
career-ops
(opens in a new tab)
is an open-source job search agent that runs in your terminal. It scores each job against your CV and tailors your resume, and you still press Submit.
Tsenta
(opens in a new tab)
is an AI career agent that finds roles that fit and applies for you.
For both sides
Jack & Jill
(opens in a new tab)
gives each side of the market its own AI agent. Jack works for the job seeker, Jill works for the company, and together they make the introduction.
For founders
Juicebox
(opens in a new tab)
and
Paraform
(opens in a new tab)
help founders hit their hiring goals: Juicebox with AI search and sourcing agents, Paraform by putting their hardest roles in front of a network of expert recruiters.
And a nod to
gstack
(opens in a new tab)
. It has nothing to do with hiring, but it is genuinely useful to builders, and that kind of usefulness builds a brand people want to work with.
