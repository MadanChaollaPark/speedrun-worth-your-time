Common questions
How many roles are open across the portfolio right now?
22,844 live roles across 761 portfolio companies, synced continuously from each company's live job board. Browse them all at https://speedrun-talent-network.com/jobs.
How many of the open roles are remote?
2,212 of the 22,844 live roles (10%) are open to remote candidates.
What do engineering roles at portfolio companies pay?
Across the 2,034 engineering roles with a posted pay band, the median band midpoint is $205k per year, and the middle half of roles fall between $170k and $250k (annual USD, computed from posted ranges only).
Which functions are hiring the most?
Engineering leads with 8,298 open roles (36% of the board), followed by operations (4,153) and sales (1,382).
Methodology: aggregates over roles currently listed on the jobs board. Pay figures use the midpoint of each posted range, annual USD only, and are shown per function, never per company. Machine-readable feeds:
